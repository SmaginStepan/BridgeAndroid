Privacy Policy – Bridge Master

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers the Bridge Master app for Android by aN0obIs ("the app"). The game is played offline on your device. This version of the app has no accounts, has no analytics of its own, and does not send any data to servers operated by us.

Stored on Your Device Only

  • Your current session, the results of finished boards and your settings.
  • A counter of finished boards, whether you have rated the app, and whether you have removed ads. These are never sent to us.

This data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Advertising

The free version may show advertisements. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about the device and about how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data and do not use it ourselves. Versions of the app used for testing may have ads switched off; in that case no advertising data is collected.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app first shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice later through the app's privacy options where offered, or by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings.
  • You can turn off ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • More about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Removing Ads (In-App Purchase)

If the app offers the one-time "Remove ads" purchase, it is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the product and remembers the result on your device so ads stay off.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No email addresses, accounts, names or passwords.
  • No contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • No analytics or behavioural-tracking SDK of our own. The only third-party SDKs that process data are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play Billing (purchases), described above.

Future Features

If a later version adds features that connect to a server (for example leaderboards, seasons or online games), we will update this policy before they are released and describe what is sent.

Your Data and Your Rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Data stored by the app lives only on your device. Data processed by Google (AdMob, Google Play) is controlled through your Google account and Android settings (see Advertising above). If you have a question about your privacy, write to the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date.
