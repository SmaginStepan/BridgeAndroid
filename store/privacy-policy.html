Privacy Policy – Bridge Master

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers the Bridge Master app for Android by aN0obIs ("the app") and the leaderboard service it can connect to at scores.preferansmaster.com. The game itself is played offline on your device. The leaderboard is optional and is off until you agree to it.

Stored on Your Device Only

  • Your current session, the results of finished boards and your settings.
  • A counter of finished boards, whether you have rated the app, whether you have removed ads, and your leaderboard choice. These are never sent to us.

This data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Top Players List (Optional)

After you finish your first 16-board session, the app asks whether you want to appear on the worldwide "Top players" list. If you say no, nothing is sent. If you say yes, then after each finished session the app sends the following to the leaderboard service so that rankings can be shown:

  • a random Player ID generated on your device (not linked to your identity, email or any account);
  • the display name you typed;
  • your session score (average matchpoint percentage) and the time it was achieved on the device.

Your display name and score are shown to other players on the list. To detect cheating, the service also records the time of every submission and the IP address it came from; the IP address is deleted after 90 days and is never shown to anyone. You can turn the list off at any time under "Top players" in the app; nothing more is sent after that. To remove what was already sent, see Delete Your Data below. All connections to the service use HTTPS, and submissions are signed.

The service is operated by us, on our own server. We do not share this data with anyone else.

Advertising

The free version shows a full-screen advertisement after some finished boards (never during the first boards, and never in the middle of a hand). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, diagnostics and crash information, and information about how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data and do not use it ourselves.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app first shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice later through "Privacy settings" in the app menu, or by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings.
  • You can turn off ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • More about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Removing Ads (In-App Purchase)

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the product and remembers the result on your device so ads stay off.

Delete Your Data

This applies to the Bridge Master app by aN0obIs. To request deletion of the data we hold about you:

  1. Email smagines@gmail.com with the subject "Bridge Master data deletion".
  2. Tell us the display name you used on the list and, if you can, roughly when you played (and your Player ID if you know it).
  3. We delete it within 30 days, normally within a few business days, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted: your entries on the list (display name, score, Player ID) and the submission log records linked to them, including the IP address.

What we do not hold: your sessions, results, settings and statistics live only on your device and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data processed by Google (AdMob, Google Play) is controlled through your Google account and Android settings (see Advertising above).

What We Do Not Collect

  • No email addresses, accounts or passwords.
  • No contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • No analytics or behavioural-tracking SDK of our own. The only third-party SDKs that process data are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play Billing (purchases), described above.

Future Features

If a later version adds other features that connect to a server (for example monthly seasons or online games), we will update this policy before they are released and describe what is sent.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only personal data we process is a device-generated random ID and a freely chosen display name, and only if you join the Top players list.

Changes

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date.
