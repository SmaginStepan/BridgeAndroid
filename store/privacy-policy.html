Bridge Master: Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Bridge Master ("the app") is a contract bridge game for Android published by [YOUR NAME OR DEVELOPER NAME] ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

1. Short version

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Your games and settings stay on your device. The only information that leaves your device is what the advertising and purchase services described below need to work.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores your game settings and progress (for example your current session and results) in the app's private storage on your device. I do not receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising

When ads are switched on, the app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is done by Google's software in the app, under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Google describes how it uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app's menu under "Privacy settings". On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

The version of the app that is used for testing may have ads switched off. In that case no advertising information is collected.

4. Purchases

If the app offers a purchase (for example to remove ads), the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. I do not receive or store your payment details. I only learn, through Google Play, that the purchase has been made, so that the app can unlock it.

5. Other services

The current version does not use accounts, analytics or crash-reporting services of its own. If a future version adds features that connect to a server (such as leaderboards or online games), this policy will be updated before they are released and will say what is sent.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets a higher age), and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices and rights

I do not hold personal information about you, so there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google, you can use the controls Google provides (see the links above and your Google account settings). If you have a question about your privacy, write to me at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
